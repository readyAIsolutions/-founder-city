The Opportunity

Why Founder City is bigger than a game

The dual identity

Every player has a Virtual Identity — net worth, companies, portfolio, negotiation ranking — and a Real Identity — actual skills, verified experience, business connections. The game turns play into a talent-discovery and networking engine.

The real-world bridge

Meet a player who runs a great virtual manufacturing company? They may be an actual manufacturer in Canada. Negotiate a supply agreement in-game, build trust through repeated transactions, then explore a real partnership.

AI that teaches

The negotiation engine doesn't just simulate — it coaches. After every deal, your AI mentor reviews your due diligence and negotiation score, showing exactly what you missed, like the hidden customer-concentration risk.

Anchored to real America

You start in your actual location. Your $5,000 is your real starting capital — relocating your business to another city costs you part of it. Geography, cost-of-living and local markets genuinely matter.

Log your listening on Rich Dad Poor Dad, Think and Grow Rich & a full curriculum (Audible-linked). Minutes earn XP + skills; finishing a book/class pays a one-time earned-money bonus and certifies your passport.

Spoken classes on sales scripts, boardroom/investor language, negotiation psychology and pitching VCs. Complete real exercises, pass, and earn cash + certified communication skills.

Built real software or a real business? Share the live link in-game. A sponsor verifies it and you earn +$3,000 and +2,500 XP — the game rewards what you actually build in reality.
